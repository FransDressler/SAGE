Problem 5.3
Main observation of the Paper
The authors examine bacterial cultures (of the species Salmonella typhimurium) at dif-
ferent bacterial growth rates μ. They measure the concentration R of different ribosomal
species (the so-called 35S, 55S, 70S and 85S sedimentation fractions) and compare them
to the concentration P of the soluble proteins (7S fraction), to obtain the ratio R/P .
They find that this ratio grows approximately proportional to μ.
