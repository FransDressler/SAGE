## Sakharov Criteria

X \rightarrow q q, \quad X \rightarrow \bar{q} \bar{q}

1) Baryon-number-violating interactions:

- Process that creates only particles (no antiparticles) and only anti-partiles (no particles)
- Lepton number violation can be translated to Baryon number violation (sphaleron processes)

2) C and CP violation:

- In addition to having a reaction that creates barons or anti-baryons only...
o ...we need a difference in the production of baryons over anti-baryons

3) Non-equilibrium situation:
o In addition to creating more baryons than anti-baryons
o ... we need a non-equilibrium, such that we do not destroy the baryons at the same rate as they are created

\Gamma(X \rightarrow q q) \neq \Gamma(\bar{X} \rightarrow \bar{q} \bar{q})
