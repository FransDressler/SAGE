6Li scintillator
• Glass material enriched with 6Li
• Neutron capture 6Li → 3H + α1 (4.78 MeV)
• Reaction products have limited range in the Li-
containing material (~ 10 μm)
• Conversion of the reaction energy into UV/visible
scintillation light → detection with regular CCD
camera (spatial resolution) or photomultiplier tube
(energy resolution).
