Phonons are quasi-particles
Quasiparticles:
•Are elementary excitations of that many-body ground state
•Behave as if they were particles with effective properties
(mass, charge, spin, etc.)
•Represent collective motion of many underlying microscopic
degrees of freedom
•Have long lifetimes (their spectral peak is sharp), meaning
interaction/scattering is weak enough to give them well-
defined energy ℏ𝜔 and momentum 𝒒
