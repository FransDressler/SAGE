01. Growth 3
Chemical vapor transport (CVT)
• Transport reaction of the form
1. AB(s) + X(g) → AX(g) + B(g)
2. Reversed reaction
• X(g) forms a volatile component with the source material AB(s).
It is called the transport agent.
• The reactants sealed in the reactor (e.g. glass ampule) and
placed in a temperature gradient.
• The growth process is completely controlled by
thermodynamics → highly repeatable and predictable.
• High quality bulk single crystals can be fabricated from lower
quality source materials, e.g. powder.
• Materials exploration → New materials can be rapidly
synthesized and their structural and chemical properties can be
characterized (X-ray crystallography, XPS analysis, etc.)
Binnewies et al. Z. Anorg. Allg. Chem. 2013, 639, (2), 219–229, Copyright Wiley 2013.

• Material growth directly from the corresponding elements.
• Ultra-high-vacuum environment (p = 10-10 mbar)
• Epitaxial growth. Vertical growth rate ~Å/min. Suitable for thin films.
• Technologically very relevant, since it provides very high material quality and
is also scalable.
