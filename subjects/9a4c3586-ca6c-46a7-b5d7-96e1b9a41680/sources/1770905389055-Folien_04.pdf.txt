Beispiel: obere Ecke in einer Einheitszelle (i.a. nicht kubisch)
![This diagram depicts a crystallographic unit cell showing the lattice vectors a, b, and c along the x, y, and z axes, with the origin labeled as "Fusspunkt" (starting point) and the opposite corner labeled as "Endpunkt" (end point). It illustrates the spatial relationship and direction within the unit cell in a 3D coordinate system.](http://localhost:5001/subjects/9a4c3586-ca6c-46a7-b5d7-96e1b9a41680/images/c585ed6b-95f0-4562-8a1f-d85f411b035d/0a367f9d-bc9f-46be-b6dd-d4863fec1b85-03_735_797_539_498.jpg)

1. Bestimme Koordinate Fuss- und Endpunkt des Vektors:

$$
x_{1}, y_{1}, z_{1} \& x_{2}, y_{2}, z_{2}
$$

2. Subtrahiere Fuss- von Endpunkt.
3. Normalisiere bzgl. Gitterkonstanten $a, b$, and $c$ :

\frac{x_{2}-x_{1}}{a} \frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{b} \frac{z_{2}-z_{1}}{c}

4. Finde kleinstes ganzzahliges Verhältnis.

\begin{aligned}
& \frac{a-0}{a}, \quad \frac{b-0}{b}, \quad \frac{c-0}{c} \\
& \Rightarrow 1,1,1 \Rightarrow \text { Richtung }\left[\begin{array}{lll}
1 & 1 & 1
\end{array}\right]
\end{aligned}
